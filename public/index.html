Frequently asked

How do I open an account?

Sign up with your email, verify your identity, and you can start saving right away.

Is my money safe?

Funds are held in segregated accounts with independent custodians. All passwords are hashed and sessions are encrypted.

Can I withdraw anytime?

Yes. Vaultra vaults have no lock-ins. Withdrawals are processed within 1–2 business days.

How do referral rewards work?

Share your unique referral link. When a friend opens an account and makes a qualifying deposit, you earn credit. Full terms are in your dashboard.

What are the fees?

Each vault displays its fee percentage before you commit. No hidden charges.

Ready to start saving?

Open your Vaultra account in under two minutes. No fees to sign up, no minimum balance.
